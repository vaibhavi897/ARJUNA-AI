NASA International Space Apps Challenge
Organising organisation
NASA, with international space-agency partners
Official link
www.spaceappschallenge.org/
Category
Open hackathon (global)
Eligibility
Open to people of all ages, skill levels and professional backgrounds. US citizenship is not required.
Academic requirements
None required. Students, developers, scientists, designers and others can join.
Team requirements
Individual or team. A third-party listing reports 1-6 members; confirm in the official participant FAQ.
Geographical restrictions
Global. Local events in many countries plus a virtual event.
Participation mode
Hybrid (in-person local events and virtual)
Themes / domains
Earth and space challenges using NASA and partner-agency open data. 2026 theme: The Next Frontier.
Rewards / prizes
Global Awards and participant certificates. Cash amounts: Not specified in the sources found.
Important requirements
Create an account on the official site, choose a challenge, submit a project. Participation is free; do not pay anyone claiming to be an organiser.
Registration timing (as of 7
Nov 2026)
2026 event: 14-15 Nov 2026. NASA Science reports registration open. Recurring every year.

2. Student-Specific Hackathons

Smart India Hackathon (SIH)
Organising organisation
Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell and AICTE
Official link
www.sih.gov.in/
Category
Student-specific; large-scale national (India)
Eligibility
Students enrolled in recognised higher-education institutions in India (engineering, management, pharmacy, architecture, design and other recognised programmes), as summarised from
the official guidelines by third-party sources.
Academic requirements
Current students. Teams must be nominated by their institution after an internal hackathon, through a registered college coordinator (SPOC).
Team requirements
Exactly 6 students from the same institution, including at least 1 female member. 1-2 mentors allowed.
Geographical restrictions
India only.
Participation mode
Grand Finale offline at nodal centres across India. Internal rounds online or offline, set by each institution.
Themes / domains
Software and hardware problem statements from ministries, PSUs and industry (for example disaster management, water, emerging technologies).
Rewards / prizes
Not verified. Check sih.gov.in.
Important requirements
Idea submission through the SIH portal; the institution must register on the portal first. Individuals cannot enter directly.
Registration timing (as of 7
Nov 2026)
2026: nomination and idea submission closed 9 Oct 2026 (extended from 30 Sep); Grand Finale proposed for Dec 2026. Recurring annually.

Google Solution Challenge - India edition
Organising organisation
Google Developer Groups on Campus (Google Developers), run in India with Hack2Skill
Official link
vision.hack2skill.com/
Category
Student-specific; large-scale national (India)
Eligibility
Participants must be 18 or older and enrolled in a college or university in India.
Academic requirements
Students of any level, beginner to advanced.
Team requirements
1-4 members. One participant can join only one team; one team submits one project.
Geographical restrictions
India for this edition.
Participation mode
Online prototype submission; later stages reported as mentoring and a final pitch.
Themes / domains
Build with AI to solve real-world problems using Google technologies (Gemini, Google Cloud, Flutter, Firebase).
Rewards / prizes
Community pages report INR 4,00,000 / 3,00,000 / 2,00,000 for the top three. Confirm officially.
Important requirements
Register individually, then form a team. Submit a working prototype with a problem statement, solution brief, demo video and code link. At least one Google technology must be used.
Free. The link shown is the host platform; Google's own programme page was not opened.
Registration timing (as of 7
Nov 2026)
2026 registration window ran about March to April 2026 and is closed. Recurring annually.

Organising organisation

Category
Company-sponsored; large-scale national (India)

Engineering students enrolled in recognised institutions in India.

Geographical restrictions

Online rounds (screening, 90-minute coding test, case-study code), then National Finals. Finals format: Not verified.
Themes / domains

Rewards / prizes
Internship and SDE-1 interview opportunities and certificates. Cash prizes: Not verified.
Important requirements
Free. Register only through Flipkart's official early-careers portal; GRiD 8.0 reportedly did not run on Unstop. The link shown is Flipkart's careers site.
Registration timing (as of 7
Nov 2026)
GRiD 8.0 registration ran from 19 June 2026; sources differ on the closing date (7 or 12 July). Next edition expected around June 2027 (third-party).

ICPC (International Collegiate Programming Contest)
Organising organisation
ICPC Foundation; Indian regional hosts include Amrita (Amritapuri), GLA University (Mathura) and CSJM University (Kanpur multi-site)
Official link
icpc.global/
Category
Software development and coding; large-scale international
Eligibility
Enrolled university students who meet the ICPC regional rules on years of higher education and prior World Finals participation. A coach certifies eligibility.
Academic requirements
University students of any branch.
Team requirements
Exactly 3 contestants plus 1 registered coach (faculty or institutional representative), from the same institution.
Geographical restrictions
Global. Indian teams compete in Indian regional contests, which can lead to the Asia West championship and the World Finals.
Participation mode
Online preliminary round, then an onsite regional contest.
Themes / domains
Algorithmic problem solving.
Rewards / prizes
Not verified overall. Amritapuri 2026 reported a INR 50,000 cash pool for women-only teams.
Important requirements
Teams register through their coach on the ICPC portal. Teams from the same university may be limited in number.
Registration timing (as of 7
Nov 2026)
India 2026 preliminaries ran on 3 Oct 2026 and registration has closed; onsite regionals fall from late Dec 2026 to Jan 2027. Recurring annually; watch icpc.global for 2027.
