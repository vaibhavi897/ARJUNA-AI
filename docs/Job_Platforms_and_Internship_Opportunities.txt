Job Platforms, Job Openings & Internship Opportunities
A resource directory for engineering students and early-career applicants | Arjuna
How to use this directory. Section 1 lists jobs and Section 2 lists internships; the two are kept separate. A platform that offers both appears in each section with its own purpose. Every link
is clickable. Links point to the platform's jobs or internships page, or the employer's careers page, wherever one exists.
About the entries. This is a directory of portals and programmes, not a list of live vacancies, because individual openings close within days or weeks. Where a specific programme was
found closed, the entry says so and the portal is listed for the next cycle. Eligibility is stated only where it was found; otherwise the entry says it varies. Always confirm eligibility and deadlines
on the official page before applying.
Safety. Genuine employers, campus drives and government recruitment do not charge a fee to apply. Apply only through the official links given here.
